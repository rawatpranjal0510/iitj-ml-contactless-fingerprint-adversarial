Overleaf project: IEEE conference paper (IEEEtran, built into Overleaf)

1. Unzip this file. Copy the PDFs from Desktop\paper\ into the figures\ folder.
2. Zip the folder again (main.tex, refs.bib and figures\ at the top level).
3. Overleaf -> New Project -> Upload Project -> choose the zip. Compiler: pdfLaTeX (default).
   Alternative: upload this zip as is, then open figures/ in Overleaf and upload the PDFs there.

Until a figure PDF is present, the paper shows a labelled box in its place, so it always compiles.
All numbers match the results report and out/tables/ on the HPC.
